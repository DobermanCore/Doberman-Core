- **Every policy now has a version.** Doberman records a `pv1:` content hash of the effective policy (mode, enforcement, preferences, egress thresholds, role globs, engine version) in a new append-only `.doberman/policies.db` every time a policy is saved or observed, linked to the ledger row that authorised it. `doberman policy-versions` lists them, `--show` prints a snapshot, `--verify` checks the catalogue, and `doberman doctor` shows the version in force. Never on the decision path; never pruned. See `docs/POLICY_VERSIONS.md`.
